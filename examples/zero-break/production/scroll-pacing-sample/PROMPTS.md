# 実際に使った作画指示

組み込み `image_gen` で、人物のいない余白用素材3枚を新規生成した。以下は実行した全文。元の装着・全身原画2枚は無加工で再利用。PNG原本の寸法・ハッシュ・出所は [assets.json](assets.json)。

## sound

```text
Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: a sparse, borderless Japanese Webtoon scroll-pacing beat, NOT a manga panel or page. Portrait 1024x1024 square. Pure flat WHITE #FFFFFF background extending to every edge. The ONLY visible content is the exact Japanese mechanical sound effect カチッ, once, near x58% y40%, occupying about 27% canvas width. Crisp dark navy hand-inked Japanese manga sound lettering, subtly angled downwards with the click, thick clean strokes, exact characters カ チ ッ. No speech balloon, no quotes, no sound lines, no repeated words. Most of the canvas is completely empty white. Letters must remain easily readable at 360px phone width. Do not add figures, hands, armor, scenery, shadows, frames, grids, panels, captions, other writing or ornament. The sound is heard from the wrist guard shown in the preceding scene; its completed armored body must remain absent.
```

## voice

```text
Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: a sparse, borderless Japanese Webtoon scroll-pacing beat containing ONLY a floating system voice/notice. Portrait 1024x1024 square. Flat pure WHITE #FFFFFF to every edge. The ONLY visible content is the EXACT Japanese line 接続完了。 near x46% y32%, ONE VERTICAL COLUMN, UPRIGHT glyphs reading from TOP to BOTTOM, no column labels or quotes. Clean bold printed Japanese gothic lettering in deep cyan-blue #087D9D. Each character about 72px high on a 1024px canvas, calm even spacing, the punctuation 。 at the lower right of its character cell according to real vertical Japanese typography. This is a short system notice after armor connection; no character is speaking on screen. Broad truly empty white space around it. No balloon, no tail, no enclosing HUD box, no glow behind text, no figure, no face, no scenery, no decorative circuitry, no extra words or captions. Render all five characters EXACTLY: 接 続 完 了 。. Keep the later completed body absent.
```

## light

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: a sparse borderless visual-only Webtoon pause before the armor reveal. Portrait tall 1024x1536. Pure flat WHITE #FFFFFF background extending to all four edges. The ONLY visible content is a single extremely slender cyan-blue energy thread: a short delicate luminous stroke begins faintly around x49% y18%, flows downward in a gently bending curve toward x44% y65%, then fades completely back into pure white by y85%. A few tiny cyan dust motes hug the thread ONLY; keep almost all canvas empty white. Polished restrained anime light rendering, white core with a narrow pale cyan halo, not a thick lightning bolt, no dark background. This carries the reader's eye downward after a system voice, while waiting to see the completed black armor in a later image. No characters, silhouettes, limbs, armor, star core, scenery, horizon, shadows, panel frame, grids, balloons, ANY text, symbols, diagrams or decorations. The top and bottom must be truly empty white so this asset joins continuous white scroll space.
