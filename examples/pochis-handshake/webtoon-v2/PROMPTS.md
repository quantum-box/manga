# v2 panel prompt set

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool. The 12 images were generated as separate panels; no external image API or API key was used. Each prompt requested one clean, full-color webtoon panel without lettering, speech balloons, sound effects, borders, or watermarks. Japanese lettering and spacing live in episode.json.

## Shared character and rendering lock

- Pochi: fluffy warm orange-red Shiba Inu; cream muzzle, chest, paws, and eyebrow spots; round dark-brown eyes; curled tail; deep teal neckerchief; one small gold star charm.
- Princess: young woman with long pale-blonde hair, silver tiara, blue eyes, and a blue-and-white gown with restrained gold embroidery.
- Demon king: charcoal-violet skin, two curved black horns, long near-black hair, violet eyes, dark iron armor with restrained violet accents.
- Polished color webtoon with expressive clean linework, soft cel shading, clear phone-size focal points, and simple backgrounds when the face or action is the focus.

## Panel prompts

1. **01-reincarnation** — Tall opening scene. A faceless human silhouette dissolves into gold star motes; the trail curves down to sleeping Pochi on moss in a dawn forest. Make the top-to-bottom reincarnation transition clear and leave quiet space for a caption.
2. **02-paw-closeup** — Extreme close-up of Pochi's cream front paw, four dark toe pads and one central pad, with startled eyes peeking over it. Keep the teal scarf and gold charm in frame.
3. **03-pochi-realizes** — Pochi's surprised face fills the frame as he studies his lifted paw. Keep the orange-red and cream markings, teal scarf, and gold star consistent; use a blurred dawn-forest backdrop.
4. **04-world-reveal** — Panoramic-feeling forest vista with mountains, water, and a distant castle. Small Pochi stands at the lower foreground looking toward the new world.
5. **05-princess-appeal** — In a bright ivory-and-blue throne hall, the pale-blonde princess kneels beside Pochi and urgently asks for help. Place her upper-left and Pochi lower-right, leaving the upper-right clear for an editable balloon.
6. **06-pochi-reply** — Tight portrait of Pochi tilting his head with a confused, friendly expression, with the royal hall softly blurred behind him and room beside his head for a balloon.
7. **07-king-foot-reveal** — Low-angle battlefield view: a huge dark armored boot dominates the foreground and tiny Pochi looks up. Hide the king's face and horns so the next scroll reveals them.
8. **08-king-looms** — Reveal the towering horned king against a violet storm sky, with long black hair, dark iron armor, and tiny Pochi at the bottom. Show an intimidating look and strong vertical scale.
9. **09-pochi-offers-paw** — Pochi calmly raises one cream front paw toward the looming king. Keep the paw separate from the king's hand and leave space beside Pochi for the short “おて。” balloon.
10. **10-king-reacts** — Silent close-up of the king's violet eyes widening and then softening as his armored hand opens palm-up and lowers. Let the storm light warm slightly.
11. **11-first-touch** — Extreme detail of one cream dog paw with four toe pads and a central pad gently touching one five-fingered black iron gauntlet. Add a small warm gold glint at contact.
12. **12-handshake** — Wide warm reveal: the king kneels and smiles while shaking Pochi's paw; Pochi wags happily and the same princess reacts with hands to her cheeks. Open the storm clouds to dawn light.

The generated raster panels are stored in panels/. A new run can vary in detail; the character notes above are repeated constraints, not a promise of pixel-identical faces.
